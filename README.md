## About Me

I am a **Senior Cloud & Platform Engineering professional** with **13 years of IT industry experience**, specializing in cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, automation and operational excellence.

My primary focus is on building and operating **scalable, secure, resilient and production-ready cloud platforms**, with a strong emphasis on Infrastructure as Code, automation, reliability and standardization.

### What I Work With

* **Google Cloud Platform** — Cloud Infrastructure & Architecture
* **Kubernetes / GKE** — Container Platforms & Workloads
* **Terraform / IaC** — Infrastructure Provisioning & Standardization
* **DevOps & CI/CD** — Automation, Deployment & Release Workflows
* **Linux / RHEL** — Enterprise Linux Engineering & Operations
* **Bash / Shell** — Infrastructure & Operational Automation
* **Docker** — Containerization
* **PostgreSQL** — Database Infrastructure
* **Observability** — Prometheus, Grafana & Elasticsearch
* **Reliability** — HA, Backup, Disaster Recovery & Operational Excellence
